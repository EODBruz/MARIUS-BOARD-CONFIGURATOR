Stick Calibration · Hardware Recenter
// writes to controller NVS

Writes the current stick rest position to the controller's permanent flash memory. Whatever the sticks are reading at the moment you click becomes the new 0, 0 center — so games and every other piece of software will see a perfectly centered stick from now on. Hands off the sticks when you click. Only works on Sony-protocol pads (DualSense / DualSense Edge, and TMR pads that pass through the Sony calibration commands). This is irreversible except by running it again with the sticks at a different rest position.

—
Stick Calibration · Range
// writes to controller NVS

The companion to Recenter. Click Start, then slowly rotate both sticks around their full outer edge a few times so the controller relearns each axis's true min/max. Click Finish & Save to commit. Sony DualSense only. Writes to permanent flash.
